What is Catalogfore?

Catalogfore is an independent Databricks cost calculator and budget planner. It models pipelines, dashboards, and AI workloads, compares named scenarios, and projects monthly and annual spend from assumptions or imported usage. It is an estimate, not a Databricks quote, and is not affiliated with Databricks.

Where should I start?

For a quick estimate, open Plan and choose your cloud, starting spend, workload mix, and monthly growth. Use my real usage imports a billing CSV; you review it before applying changes. Advanced configures individual products, machines, quantities, and schedules. These are different starting points for the same forecast. Use the Plan section links to move between Inputs, Forecast, and Budget and scenarios.

What does the DBU forecast include?

Configuration estimates price Databricks usage and the relevant cloud infrastructure. Usage imports preserve the selected month’s exported monetary costs. Missing DBU prices use labelled catalog estimates; unsupported charges remain visibly unpriced. A virtual machine bill is not inferred from imported DBUs.

How is this different from a Databricks pricing estimator?

Catalogfore combines mixed workloads into a plan over time, with budgets, dated changes, and named scenarios. It can read local billing CSV files and keeps the calculation in the browser. It does not require a workspace login or a connected billing account.

How do Product map and Unit costs fit into my plan?

Product map explains services, platform concepts, and example architectures. Select an entry, then Inspect unit costs to check its billing components and source evidence. In Unit costs, choose a rate and try a billable quantity. Browsing and calculating leave your plan unchanged; Add to forecast or Update forecast applies a supported quantity. Product map can also add supported products with defaults and open Advanced so you can review their configuration.

Which usage files can I import?

Open Use my real usage in Plan and choose a Databricks billing CSV or a recognized FOCUS cost and usage CSV in USD, up to 8 MB. The import guide shows how to prepare a file, and demonstration samples let you explore first. Review the selected month, cost basis, price coverage, and warnings before replacing or adding workloads. The latest closed calendar month is selected by default. FOCUS effective and billed costs are labelled separately.

Can I compare scenarios against a budget?

Open Budget and scenarios from the Plan section links or below the forecast charts. Set an annual budget, save a named baseline, change the plan, then select the baseline under Compare current plan with. The charts show the comparison without replacing your current plan. Open a saved scenario to make it the active plan. Up to 20 scenarios stay in this browser; export each active plan as JSON for a portable backup.

Can I save, reopen, and share a plan?

Copy link creates a link to the current plan, without usage attribution summaries or your saved scenario library. Anyone with the link can read it. If a plan is too large for a link, keep the page open and choose Export → JSON. JSON saves the active plan so it can be reopened with Menu → Open forecast, including in another browser. It does not back up all saved scenarios at once. XLSX, CSV, and PDF are reports; use JSON to continue editing. Imported forecast JSON files must be no larger than 8 MB.

What do the charts and forecast range mean?

The chart plots imported observed costs in USD alongside the model’s future projection. DBU-only observations appear in the history table. The low-to-high planning range combines usage uncertainty and pricing assumptions; it is not a calibrated probability interval. Unpriced charges remain outside the known cost subtotal.

Are prices live or official quotes?

Prices are a bundled snapshot, not live quotes. Unit costs separates official list prices, derived values, planning estimates, unresolved prices, and platform context. Open a rate’s Source evidence for its billing scope, observation date, and review date. Reviewing a source does not refresh an older price, and a listed price does not confirm regional availability. Check current provider pricing and your contract before making a purchasing decision; taxes and separately billed charges may be excluded.

Does Catalogfore upload a usage file or the estimate?

No. Usage files and calculations stay in this browser; Catalogfore does not upload them to a planning server or connect to your billing account. A share link embeds the current plan in its URL hash, so anyone receiving it can read that plan. Usage attribution summaries are excluded from links; workload names and other plan content can still be included. Saved JSON files can contain team or owner labels. Review a link or file before sharing it.
