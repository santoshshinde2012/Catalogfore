Questions about the forecast

Cayfore is a Databricks cost calculator and DBU forecast. These answers describe the estimate. They are not a Databricks quote, and Cayfore is not affiliated with Databricks.

What is Cayfore?

Cayfore is an independent Databricks cost calculator. It forecasts a year of DBU and cloud spend from four inputs: cloud and tier, a starting amount or team size, a mix of pipelines, dashboards, and models, and monthly growth. It is an estimate, not a Databricks quote, and it is not affiliated with Databricks.

What does the DBU forecast include?

The forecast prices Databricks usage, measured in DBU, and the cloud machines under those workloads for 12 months. The default is AWS Premium in US East with 3 percent monthly growth: about $170,258 a year. A usage file can replace the starting amount. The year is shown as a low-to-high range.

How is this different from a Databricks pricing estimator?

A vendor pricing estimator prices one product configuration. Cayfore prices a year of mixed workloads — pipelines, dashboards, and models — and keeps the calculation in the browser. It does not sign in to a workspace and it does not read an invoice.

Does Cayfore upload a usage file or the estimate?

No. The usage file stays in this browser. The share link is only the URL hash. Cayfore does not save the estimate on a server.
